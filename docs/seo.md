# SEO Notes

- Nuxt SSR/hybrid rendering is the default. Keep commercial and local SEO content server-rendered.
- Route pages live under `/morskie-progulki/:slug/` and `/rybalka/:slug/` and use `TouristTrip` schema.
- Informational guide pages live under `/guides/:slug/`, render from `data/guide-pages.ts`, and funnel users to commercial service URLs through SSR internal links.
- The home page uses `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, `TravelAgency` and `FAQPage` schema.
- Sitemap URLs are generated by `server/utils/sitemap-urls.ts`; the same source feeds Nuxt Sitemap at build time and the stable `/api/__sitemap__/urls` endpoint.
- `robots.txt` is generated by `server/routes/robots.txt.ts` from `NUXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`.
- AI agent discovery files live at `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt`.
- Image optimization plan: `docs/image-optimization.md`.
- Use natural local terms: Камчатка, Петропавловск-Камчатский, Авачинская бухта, морские прогулки, глубоководная рыбалка, крабовое сафари.
- Fresh photo pages and reviews should include dates, route names, alt text, and moderation status.
